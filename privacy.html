Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This Google OAuth client is used only by its owner (resulcakir) to access their own Google Drive account through rclone running on their own computers.

Data accessed

The client requests access to Google Drive so that rclone can list, read, upload and modify the owner's files. Files are transferred directly between Google Drive and the owner's devices.

Data collection and sharing

No data is collected, stored on any server, sold or shared with third parties. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's devices.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Via the owner's GitHub profile: github.com/resulcakir.
